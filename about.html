About

Who I am

Robotics engineer specialising in deep learning for robotic perception and vision-based manipulation. I build end-to-end pick-and-place systems on industrial robot arms, combining 2D/3D object detection, segmentation, 6-DoF pose estimation, grasp planning, and real-time force-based control.

Grounded in robot kinematics, dynamics and control from an electromechanical engineering background. Experienced in evaluating and calibrating sensors for high-quality training data, validating algorithms on real-world datasets, and documenting systems for reproducible deployment.

Currently Founding Robotics AI Engineer & Tech Lead at Motion One, owning the full perception-to-manipulation stack from pre-seed onward. Previously built the intelligent grasping framework at imec in Brussels.
